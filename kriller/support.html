KRILLER · SUPPORT

HOW TO PLAY

Five calls a day, the same for everyone. You get two seconds to read each call, then 25 seconds to type one answer. Rarer answers lower Pip farther down the cable: Ripple +25m, Pulse +50m, Surge +75m, Roar +100m. A new dive starts at 00:00 UTC.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY

Pip Party: 2 to 6 players pass one phone; matching answers score 0. Themed packs: ten untimed calls each; Deep Sea is free. Archive: play past dives. The other packs and the Archive come with KRILLER Plus.

MY ANSWER WASN’T ACCEPTED

KRILLER only accepts answers on that day’s sheet. Close spellings are offered as you type. If you think a correct answer is missing, email us and we’ll add it to future sheets.

RESTORE KRILLER PLUS

Open the menu (top-left ☰), tap Plus, then “Restore purchases”, signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.
