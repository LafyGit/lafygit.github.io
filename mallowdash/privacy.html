MALLOW DASH · PRIVACY

Last updated 28 November 2026. Mallow Dash is made by Lafy AlMutlaq.

THE SHORT VERSION

Mallow Dash does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and the app does not connect to the internet.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your high score, best distance, best combo, totals, achievements, chosen Mallow style, tutorial progress and sound, music and haptics settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

SHARING

If you tap Share after a run, iOS opens its own share sheet with a short message about your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it, and that goes through the app you pick, not through us.

CHILDREN

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

YOUR CHOICES

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
