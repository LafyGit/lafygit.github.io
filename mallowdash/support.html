HOW TO PLAY

Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump over licorice and wafers, and swipe down to slide under candy arches. Collect Sparkles to build your combo up to ×5. Fill the Candy Charge meter to set off a Candy Burst: for a few seconds your score doubles and you can smash through hazards.

POWER-UPS

Bubble Shield saves you from one hit. Sprinkle Magnet pulls Sparkles to you. Slow Swirl slows the world down. Rainbow Ribbon gives bonus points and a big boost to your Candy Charge. Golden Gumdrops are worth 500 points, multiplied by your combo.

MALLOW STYLES

Berry unlocks at a 1,000 score, Mint after collecting 250 Sparkles in total, and Midnight at a 10,000 score.

SOUND AND HAPTICS

Tap the gear on the home screen to turn sound effects, the Burst music layer and haptics on or off, or to replay the tutorial.

MY PROGRESS IS GONE

Progress is stored only on your device, so it is removed if you delete the app and it does not move to a new phone.
