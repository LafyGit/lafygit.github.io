Crumbless

A gluten-free and allergy companion for iPhone, in English and Arabic. Scan a pack and see whether it fits your needs, learn what each diet really means, and cook from a recipe book that fits you.

Crumbless is not medical advice. Packs change, so read the label every time.

Support

Found a problem, a wrong verdict, or have an idea? Email lafyalmutlaq@gmail.com. If it is about a product, include its barcode.

Your data

Your diet and scans stay on your phone. Open your profile and tap Delete my data, then Delete everything.

Product information

Labels come from Open Food Facts, an open database anyone can improve (ODbL).
