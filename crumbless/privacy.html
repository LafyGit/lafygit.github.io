Crumbless

Privacy Policy

Crumbless for iPhone · Last updated 28 November 2026

The short version

Crumbless has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of ours. What you tell it about your diet, what you scan, your food and symptom notebook and your cycle tracking stay on your iPhone. We do not collect any of it and cannot see it.

What stays on your iPhone

Your name (if you give one), dietary needs and allergies, how strict you want checks to be, your scan history, product labels saved for offline use, corrections you make to a product, your notebook (what you ate, how you felt), cycle tracking if you turn it on (period dates, flow, symptoms), posts, reviews or places you write, your profile picture if you pick one, and your settings. All of it is kept in the app's own storage on the device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What goes over the internet

Barcode lookups. When you scan or type a barcode, the app asks Open Food Facts, a non-profit open food database, for that product's label, and loads the product photo from its image server. The request carries the barcode only: no name, no account, no location, no diet. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy covers that request.

Links you open. Some recipes credit their creator with a link. If you tap one, it opens that website, which has its own privacy policy.

Nothing else leaves the phone. There is no tracking, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party analytics.

Camera

The scanner uses the camera only to read barcodes. Frames are read on the phone and thrown away; no picture is stored or sent.

Location

Places can sort its list by distance. If you allow it, the app reads your location while it is open, uses it on the phone to sort, and never stores or sends it.

Sharing

If you choose to share a recipe, a note or a doctor summary of your notebook, the iOS share sheet opens and you decide where it goes. Your cycle is only included if you tick it for that one share.

Health information

Crumbless shows what a product's label data says against the needs you picked. It is not medical advice. Product data is entered by the Open Food Facts community and packs change, so always read the label on the pack in your hand.

Backups

Because your data lives in the app's storage, it is part of your iPhone's normal backup (iCloud or computer), protected the way Apple protects backups.

Deleting your data

In the app: tap your name at the top, then Privacy & data, then Delete my data. Deleting the app also deletes everything it kept. There is nothing on our side to delete.

Children

Crumbless is not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts or sends any of your data off the phone, this policy will be updated before that version ships and the app will ask you first.
