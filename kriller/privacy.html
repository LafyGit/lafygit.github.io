KRILLER · PRIVACY

Last updated 27 November 2026. KRILLER is made by Lafy Tafy Productions.

THE SHORT VERSION

You can play without an account, a name or an email. Your daily dive, tapes, streak and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run a server and we never sell anything.

ADS

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. On first launch iOS asks whether KRILLER may track you. If you say no, ads are still shown but are not personalised with your advertising identifier. Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier (only if you allow it), IP-derived coarse location, ad interactions and diagnostics. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying KRILLER Plus removes all ads.

PURCHASES

Purchases are handled by Apple. The app checks and restores KRILLER Plus with Apple directly, on your device. We never see your card details or your Apple ID.

CHILDREN

KRILLER is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

YOUR CHOICES

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. You can change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
