Crumbless

Support

Crumbless checks food labels against what you avoid: gluten, allergens and other diets. It keeps a private food and symptom notebook and optional cycle tracking, all on your iPhone.

Scanning

Tap the red Scan button and point the camera at the barcode. If the camera cannot read it, type the number under the viewfinder, or try one of the sample products listed there. Product data comes from Open Food Facts; if a product is missing, it has not been added there yet.

A verdict looks wrong

After a scan, answer "Does this match your pack?" with No and correct it. A correction can only make a verdict more careful, never less. Verdicts come from label data and are not medical advice: always read the pack.

Changing what you avoid

Tap your name at the top, then Edit.

Places, Friends and Order in

In this version these show examples to explain the idea. The places, people, reviews and prices in them are made up and marked as examples in the app.

Deleting your data

Tap your name, then Privacy & data, then Delete my data. Nothing is stored on any server. See the privacy policy.
