Crumbless

Privacy policy

Crumbless for iPhone · Last updated 21 November 2026 · العربية

The short version

Crumbless has no account and no server. What you tell it about your diet, and what you scan, stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and nobody else can either.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything below lives only on your phone, in the app's own storage.

WhatWhy the app needs itLeaves the phone?
Your dietary needs, how careful you asked us to be, and anything else you are avoidingTo check each product against your needs, not a defaultNever
The barcodes you scanned, when, and how many timesSo recent scans are one tap awayNever
Product labels fetched from Open Food FactsSo a product you scanned before still works with no signalNever
Corrections you made ("the pack says it contains…")So your correction applies next time you scan that packOnly if a later version offers sharing and you turn it on
Your language, theme, and whether you have seen the tourSo the app opens the way you left itNever

A dietary profile is health information, and a list of barcodes scanned over time can reveal the same thing. The app's code marks both as data that may never be sent anywhere, and checks that rule every time it writes to storage.

What the app sends over the internet

Barcode lookups. When you scan, the app asks Open Food Facts for that barcode's label. Open Food Facts is a non-profit open database. The request carries the barcode and nothing about you: no identifier, no account, no location, no diet. Their privacy policy applies to that request: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Nothing else. There is no analytics service, no advertising, no crash reporter and no account system in this version. Links to recipe sources open in your browser only when you tap them.

Usage counters

To see whether the app works (for example, how often a scan finds nothing), the app keeps a few weekly tallies on the phone. They cannot contain a barcode, a dietary need, an email address, a location or free text. They are not sent anywhere in this version. If a future version offers to share them, it will ask first, and the answer will default to no.

Camera and location

The scanner uses the camera to read barcodes. Frames are processed on the phone and discarded; no image is stored or sent. Where the app sorts places by distance, it uses your location only while the app is open and only if you allow it; the coordinates are not stored or sent.

Backups

Because the data lives in the app's storage, it is part of your phone's normal backup (iCloud or computer), under the protection Apple applies to backups.

Deleting your data

In the app, open your profile and tap Delete my data, then Delete everything. It removes your needs, scan history, saved labels and corrections from the phone. There is nothing on our side to delete, because nothing was sent to us. Deleting the app also deletes it all.

Children

Crumbless is not directed at children under 13, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds an account, sharing, or any transfer of data off the phone, this policy will change before that version ships, the app will ask for your consent, and saying no will not remove any existing feature.
